## Lab-wide deviations

- **Sinks:** CAMARA requires `https` sinks; the lab accepts `http` (`notify.allow_http_sink`).
- **Roaming:** UERANSIM can't roam; roaming scenarios use the lab override for the visited PLMN.
- **QoD radio leg:** UERANSIM's gNB drops N2 on `PDUSessionResourceModifyRequest`; the QoS flow is verified in the core (SMF/PCF), not on the radio.
- **Swap history:** starts when the gateway starts monitoring (no historic SIM/device data in Open5GS).
- **QoD duration out of range:** returns `INVALID_ARGUMENT` per the r4.3 test definitions (the OpenAPI also lists `QUALITY_ON_DEMAND.DURATION_OUT_OF_RANGE`).
